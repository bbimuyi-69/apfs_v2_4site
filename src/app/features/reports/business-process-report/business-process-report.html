Business Process Report
This report shows business process activity by workflow section and may be filtered based on the selected components, offices, date range or fiscal year. If none of the filter criteria are selected, the report will return results based on all records in 4SITE. Hyperlinks are provided to lists of the individual records for approvals, rejections, current records and touched records. The results are downloadable as CSV, Excel and PDF. Once the report is run the criteria can be updated by clicking on the "Show Filters" button, changing the desired criteria, and clicking the "Filter Report" button.
