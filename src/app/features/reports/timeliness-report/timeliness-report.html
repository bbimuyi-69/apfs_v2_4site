Timeliness Report
This report shows how much time prior to their anticipated award date records are published, and may be filtered based on the selected components, offices, date range or fiscal year. If none of the filter criteria are selected, the report will return results based on all records in APFS. The results will return a bar chart showing the percentage of records published after the award date and within 3, 6, 9, 12 and more than a year of the anticipated award date. Hyperlinks are provided to lists of the individual records in each range, the results are downloadable as CSV, Excel and PDF. The Excel provides the most detailed results, while the PDF and CSV mirror what is available on the screen. Once the report is run the criteria can be updated by clicking on the "Show Filters" button, changing the desired criteria, and clicking the "Filter Report" button. Clicking "Home" or the APFS Icon will return you to the Dashboard.
