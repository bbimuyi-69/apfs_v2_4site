The Department of Homeland Security (DHS) Forecasting System for Industry Tracking and Engagement (4SITE) is a government portal used to create and track new contract actions over $350,000 that small businesses may be able to perform under direct contracts with DHS and includes procurements expected to have a solicitation released in the current and/or subsequent fiscal years.

Sign In Request Account

All projected procurements are subject to revision or cancellation. The forecast data is for planning purposes and does not commit the Government to purchase the desired products and services. 4SITE is not authorized for the processing or storage of classified or sensitive information.

For additional information on procurements not expected to exceed $350,000, please contact the DHS Small Business Specialist for your Component.
