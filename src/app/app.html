    About This Data

    Public Law 100-656, the Business Opportunity Development Reform Act of 1988, amended the Small Business Act to emphasize acquisition planning. The law requires agencies to compile and make projections of contracting opportunities that small businesses (including small, disadvantaged businesses 8(a), women-owned, HUBZone, veteran-owned, and service-disabled veteran-owned) may perform. For more details, review the 'About' link above.

    Potential Subcontracting Opportunities

    The information in APFS helps build the statutorily required annual Forecast of Contract Opportunities (i.e. the Forecast). The Forecast allows industry, especially small businesses, the opportunity to learn about prospective DHS business opportunities (including subcontracting) over the simplified acquisition threshold (SAT).
